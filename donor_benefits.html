About Us

Palavi Samajik Sanstha is dedicated to empowering women, caring for the elderly, and providing matrimonial aid to those in need. This page outlines the benefits you receive when you contribute to our cause and become a valued donor. Your support helps us continue our essential work in communities across India.

General Donor Benefits

Tax Deduction

  • Donations to Palavi Samajik Sanstha are eligible for tax deduction under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act
  • Receive a 50% deduction on your donation amount (up to 10% of your adjusted gross total income)
  • Requirements: Non-cash payment, filing of Income Tax Return (ITR), and valid donation receipt

Social Impact

Your contribution directly supports our programs:

  • Skill development and vocational training for women
  • Healthcare services and support for the elderly
  • Counseling and matrimonial assistance for underprivileged individuals
  • Community development initiatives that create lasting change

Recognition

  • Personalized thank-you certificates for all donations
  • Acknowledgment in our annual reports and website (as per our donor recognition policy)
  • Process: Recognition is typically processed within 2-3 weeks of donation confirmation

Personal Satisfaction

  • Alignment with social good and meaningful contribution to society
  • Sense of fulfillment from making a tangible difference in people's lives
  • Connection to a community of like-minded individuals committed to positive change

Example: INR 5,000 Donation

Here's an example of the benefits you would receive with a donation of INR 5,000:

Tax Deduction

  • INR 2,500 deduction (50% of INR 5,000)
  • Example scenario: If your income is INR 8,50,000, your qualifying donation limit would be INR 85,000 (10% of income)
  • How to avail: Make payment via non-cash methods, retain the receipt we provide, and claim the deduction when filing your ITR

Social Impact

Your INR 5,000 contribution can help us:

  • Provide vocational training to 10 women, empowering them toward financial independence
  • Conduct health check-ups for 5 elderly individuals who lack access to regular healthcare
  • Offer counseling sessions to those seeking matrimonial assistance and support

Recognition

  • Personalized certificate of appreciation
  • Mention in our quarterly donor report
  • Possible inclusion in our website's donor appreciation section (with your permission)
  • Process: You'll receive your certificate within 2 weeks of donation confirmation

Personal Satisfaction

  • Pride in knowing you've helped empower women to achieve financial independence
  • Satisfaction from supporting elderly individuals who need care and attention
  • Fulfillment from contributing to stronger families through matrimonial support services

Important Notes

  • 80G Verification: You can verify our 80G certification on NGO Darpan or by contacting us at info@palavisanstha.org
  • Donation Process: Donations can be made through our website palavisanstha.org or by contacting us via email
  • Verification: We encourage donors to verify our legitimacy and credentials before donating. All our registration information is available on our website.
